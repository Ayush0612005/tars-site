TARS privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Who uses TARS

TARS is a personal tool used only by its developer, on his own computer. No one else can sign in to it, and it is not distributed.

Google data accessed

  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly): message headers, snippets and text.
  • Google Calendar, read-only (calendar.readonly): upcoming events.

TARS cannot send, modify or delete email or calendar events.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is processed on the owner's computer to decide what needs his attention.
  • Email content is analysed by a model running locally on that computer.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in the operating system's credential store.
  • Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Limited use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
